# Design Constraints & Global Rules

## Global Balance Rules

Level-20 power growth must remain under 2.6× baseline.

Soft caps must always limit scaling.

Specialization must remain viable but not dominant.

No equipment piece may define >15% total power alone.

Combat must remain readable and predictable.
