# Skill Trees

## Purpose

Skill trees are the main “build transcendence system.”

Classes create structure and identity; skill trees create:

- player agency
- long-term build expression
- cross-class experimentation
- late-game identity refinement

---

## Investment Model

By Level 20, a player can invest:

- **2 trees major**
    - unlock deep nodes + capstone tier
    - capstone choice: **select 1 of 3 capstones per tree**
- **1 tree minor**
    - used for refinement, patching weaknesses, hybrid flavor

This structure ensures:

- builds remain distinct
- decisions remain flexible across the journey
- late-game synergies are earned, not rushed

---

## Tree Themes (Current Set)

- **Valor** — durability, stubborn survival, defensive spikes
- **Instinct** — movement, reaction, dodge mastery, tempo control
- **Discipline** — weapon mastery, stamina economy, precision efficiency
- **Intellect** — arcane power, mana loops, spell enhancement
- **Zeal** — sustain, resilience, anti-debuff, holy retaliation
- **Ferocity** — aggressive pressure, execute windows, berserker tempo

(Additional trees can be added later if needed.)

---

## Skill Power Equivalence Rules

Skill nodes convert into “effective stat value” and/or mechanics.

Baseline equivalence:

- Minor node ≈ +2 to +3 stat value
- Major node ≈ +5 to +8 stat value
- Capstone ≈ +12 to +18 stat value

Purpose:

- skill investment must compete with stat investment
- but not override it completely

---

## Major Tree Packages (Stat + Mechanics)

These are the “capstone-complete identity packages” we used in current prototypes.

### Valor (Major)

Stats:

- +6 CON
- +5 DEF

Mechanics examples:

- Above 70% HP: take 10% less damage
- Debuff durations reduced
- “Second Wind” once per match

---

### Instinct (Major)

Stats:

- +6 SPD
- +5 DEX

Mechanics examples:

- After a successful dodge: +crit chance window
- Faster dodge recovery
- Tempo bonuses for perfect timing

---

### Discipline (Major)

Stats:

- +6 STR
- +4 DEX

Mechanics examples:

- stamina costs reduced
- weapon scaling enhanced
- combo control tools

---

### Intellect (Major)

Stats:

- +7 ARC
- +5 MR

Mechanics examples:

- spell hits restore mana
- spell effects gain secondary behavior
- improved mana efficiency

---

### Zeal (Major)

Stats:

- +6 FTH
- +5 CON

Mechanics examples:

- healing amplification
- debuff reduction
- retaliatory holy damage

---

### Ferocity (Major)

Stats:

- +7 STR
- +5 SPD

Mechanics examples:

- damage bonus vs wounded targets
- stamina penalty suppression
- short “execute windows”

---

## Minor Investment Model (Refinement)

Minor tree investment provides:

- +3 to the tree’s primary stat
- +2 to the tree’s secondary stat
- a small mechanical perk

Example:

Minor Instinct:

- +3 SPD
- +2 DEX
- “+5% dodge recovery speed”

---

## Capstone Philosophy (3 Choices per Tree)

Each tree should end with 3 capstones that define *how* you play, not just raw power. These cost 2 skill points to unlock, and are locked behind Minimum Stat Requirements (see fig. 2 below). 

Example: Instinct capstones could be:

- Counterstrike tempo (reward perfect dodges)
- Mobility supremacy (movement control)
- Assassin burst (crit windows)

---

## Design constraints

- Capstones should not invalidate class identity.
- Capstones should create new patterns, not raw stat inflation.
- Most capstones should be tuned via mechanics, not huge stats.
