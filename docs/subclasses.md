# Crucible Gladiator System

## Emergent Subclass Archetypes by Class

### Based on Weighted Base Stat Generation

This document outlines the **most statistically likely subclass archetypes** that emerge naturally from the current class-based weighted stat generation system.

These subclasses are **not forced builds**. They represent the most probable combinations of dominant stats that arise from:

* Class weight distributions
* Base stat minting
* Typical player leveling reinforcement
* Functional PvP combat identities

Each class produces three primary emergent subclasses, for a total of **15 dominant archetypes** across the Gladiator population.

---

# DUELIST

### Core Identity

Precision, speed, tempo control, and evasive combat.

### Natural Stat Tendencies

* Very high **DEX**
* High **SPD**
* Moderate **CON**
* Moderate **STR**

Duelists statistically trend toward **mobility-driven offensive builds** with varying durability investments.

---

## 1. Tempo Striker (DEX + SPD)

**Most common Duelist outcome**

### Combat Identity

High-evasion burst attacker focused on tempo advantage.

### Play Pattern

* Frequent dodge interactions
* Rapid engagement / disengagement
* Precision burst windows
* Movement-based damage scaling

### PvP Strengths

* Difficult to hit
* Controls pacing of fight
* Strong punish capability

### PvP Weaknesses

* Vulnerable to control denial
* Limited sustain if pressured continuously

---

## 2. Endurance Fencer (DEX + CON)

**Common durable Duelist variant**

### Combat Identity

Precision duelist with defensive stability.

### Play Pattern

* Sustained pressure
* Counter-focused engagements
* Survives longer exchanges
* Reliable mechanical consistency

### PvP Strengths

* High survivability relative to Duelist baseline
* Excellent in extended duels
* Strong reactive gameplay

### PvP Weaknesses

* Lower burst ceiling than Tempo Striker
* Can be outpaced by extreme mobility builds

---

## 3. Skirmisher (SPD + STR)

**Mobility-driven aggression build**

### Combat Identity

Fast pursuit attacker with heavier impact.

### Play Pattern

* Gap closing pressure
* Chase-down finishing
* Aggressive engagement loops
* Momentum-based offense

### PvP Strengths

* Strong execution pressure
* Difficult disengagement for opponents
* High chase lethality

### PvP Weaknesses

* Lower precision scaling
* Relies heavily on engagement success
